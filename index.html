Shortest Stop Route

Enter up to 12 stops. The route starts at your current address and visits every stop with the least total driving.

Need gas or lunch? Type a name like Shell gas station, Wendy's or lunch on the line after a stop. It stays right after that stop and uses the location with the least detour toward the next one. Start with any, like any Kroger, to let it go wherever fits best. Add a time after @, like @ by 5pm or @ 2-4pm.
